Buyer’s Guide

Travel Insurance From The UAE: What You Need To Know

Do I Need Travel Insurance For A Schengen Visa?

Yes. Schengen embassies require travel medical insurance with at least EUR 30,000 of cover for emergency medical treatment and repatriation, valid across all Schengen states for the full length of your stay. Our Schengen Basic plan, and every plan that covers Europe, meets this requirement.

What Does Travel Insurance Cover?

Travel insurance protects you against the unexpected while you’re away: emergency medical treatment and evacuation, trip cancellation or curtailment, flight delays, lost or delayed baggage, a lost passport or money, and personal liability if you accidentally injure someone or damage their property.

Should I Choose Single Trip Or Annual Multi-Trip?

Single Trip cover is designed for one journey. Annual Multi-Trip may suit people who travel several times during the year. Check the policy wording for trip duration limits, destinations and eligibility before you buy.

What Information Do You Need For A Quote?

Have each traveller’s details, destination and travel dates ready. Choose the plan that fits your trip and review the optional benefits before completing your purchase.

FAQ’s

Travel Insurance Questions, Answered

01What do I need to buy travel insurance?+

Have each traveller’s details, destination and travel dates ready before you start your quote.

02Is there an age limit?+

Age limits and eligibility depend on the plan. Check the policy wording or contact us before you buy if you have questions about your trip.

03Can I buy travel insurance after I’ve left the UAE?+

Travel insurance must be purchased before you leave the UAE. You cannot buy a policy after your trip has started.

04How long can a single trip be?+

Single Trip cover can be for up to 92 days, subject to the plan terms and eligibility requirements.

05Can I add winter sports or adventure activities?+

Optional benefits may be available for selected activities and plans. Activity restrictions and conditions apply, so check the policy wording before your trip.

06How do I make a travel insurance claim?+

Contact the assistance team listed in your policy where required, then submit the claim form with supporting reports and receipts.
