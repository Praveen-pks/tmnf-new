Buyer’s Guide

Car Insurance In Dubai And The UAE: What You Need To Know

Is Car Insurance Mandatory In The UAE?

Yes. Every vehicle registered in the UAE must have at least Third Party liability insurance before it can be registered or renewed with the traffic authority. Driving without valid insurance can lead to fines, black points and the vehicle being impounded.

Comprehensive Or Third Party: Which Is Right For You?

Third Party cover pays for damage or injury you cause to other people and their property. Comprehensive cover, like Tokio Must, also protects your own car against accidental damage, fire, theft, storms and floods, and adds benefits such as roadside assistance, a rental car and personal accident cover. For newer or financed cars, comprehensive cover is usually the better choice.

What Documents Do You Need To Get Insured?

Have your Emirates ID, driving licence and vehicle registration card (Mulkiya) ready. You may also need previous policy details if you are renewing or transferring your no-claims discount.

What Should You Do After An Accident?

Move to a safe place, report the accident to the police and keep a copy of the police report. Then register your claim with your insurer and share your policy details, the report and photos of the damage.

FAQ’s

Car Insurance Questions, Answered

01What do I need to buy car insurance in Dubai?+

Have your Emirates ID, driving licence and vehicle registration card (Mulkiya) ready. You can get a quote online and choose the cover that suits your car.

02Will I get a motor quote if I hold a foreign licence?+

Eligibility depends on the vehicle and policy requirements. Contact us with your details and we can help you find the available options.

03Why is the validity of my car insurance 13 months?+

Motor policies in the UAE commonly include a 13-month period to provide cover through the vehicle registration renewal cycle. Check your policy schedule for the exact dates.

04Can I get comprehensive insurance for my old vehicle?+

Comprehensive cover may be available depending on your vehicle’s age, condition and underwriting requirements. Request a quote to check your options.

05Can insurance and registration be in two different names?+

The policyholder and registered owner details usually need to meet the insurer’s requirements. Contact our team to confirm what applies to your vehicle.

Tokio Marine offers a range of business insurance products. Contact our team to discuss the cover available for your business.
