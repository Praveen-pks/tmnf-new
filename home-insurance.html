Buyer’s Guide

Home insurance in Dubai and the UAE: what you need to know

Is home insurance mandatory in the UAE?

Home insurance isn’t required by law, but mortgage lenders usually require building cover, and many landlords and owners’ associations recommend or require it. Without it, the cost of repairs, replacing belongings or a liability claim falls entirely on you.

Building cover or contents cover: what’s the difference?

Building cover protects the structure of your home and its permanent fixtures, such as walls, roof, kitchen fittings and bathrooms. Contents cover protects what you’d take with you if you moved: furniture, electronics, clothing, jewellery and other personal belongings.

Should I Choose Single Trip Or Annual Multi-Trip?

Single Trip cover is designed for one journey. Annual Multi-Trip may suit people who travel several times during the year. Check the policy wording for trip duration limits, destinations and eligibility before you buy.

What Information Do You Need For A Quote?

Have each traveller’s details, destination and travel dates ready. Choose the plan that fits your trip and review the optional benefits before completing your purchase.

FAQ’s

Home Insurance Questions, Answered

01What do I need to buy home insurance?+

Have your contact details, the property address and type, and the value of the building and contents you want to cover ready before you start your quote.

02Can I insure a property I don’t live in?+

Speak to us about properties you let out or that are empty for a period. Eligibility and cover depend on how the property is used. Call 800 8663 for help.

03Do you need to inspect my home before I buy?+

Most homes can be insured without an inspection. For higher-value properties or contents, we may ask for more details before we confirm cover.

04Do you cover villas and apartments in all emirates?+

Yes. We insure villas and apartments across the UAE, subject to the plan terms and eligibility requirements.

05Can tenants buy home insurance?+

Yes. Renters can cover their contents and tenant’s liability without insuring the building itself.

06Can I insure valuables?+

Yes. Tell us about higher-value items and choose the options that fit your needs.

07Is emergency assistance included?+

Yes. Every plan includes 24/7 home emergency assistance; check your policy schedule for the details.

08How do I make a claim?+

Contact Tokio Marine with your policy details and information about what happened. Our team will guide you through the next steps.
