Buyer’s Guide

Corporate Motor Insurance in the UAE: what you need to know

What is Corporate Motor Insurance?

Corporate Motor Insurance covers all the vehicles a business owns or leases under one policy, instead of separate policies for each vehicle. It is simpler to manage and usually more cost-effective once you run two or more vehicles.

Is fleet insurance mandatory in the UAE?

Every vehicle registered in the UAE must have at least third party liability insurance. A fleet policy meets that requirement for every vehicle on the schedule, and comprehensive cover adds

Should I Choose Single Trip Or Annual Multi-Trip?

Single Trip cover is designed for one journey. Annual Multi-Trip may suit people who travel several times during the year. Check the policy wording for trip duration limits, destinations and eligibility before you buy.

What Information Do You Need For A Quote?

Have each traveller’s details, destination and travel dates ready. Choose the plan that fits your trip and review the optional benefits before completing your purchase.

Corporate Motor questions, answered

01What information will you ask for?+

We’ll ask about your vehicles, how they are used, your drivers and recent claims. A vehicle schedule and current policy details can help us prepare your quote.
